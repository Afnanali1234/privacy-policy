PawCare ("PawCare", "we", "our", or "us") is a pet health and wellness application designed to help pet owners manage pet profiles, medications, schedules, health timelines, veterinary contacts, and other pet-care information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information PawCare collects, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

By using PawCare, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

PawCare collects information that you provide when creating an account and using the application's features.

1.1 Account and Profile Information

When you create and use a PawCare account, we may collect information such as:

  • Email address
  • Name or display name
  • Profile information you provide
  • Profile picture, if you choose to upload one
  • Authentication-related information required to maintain your PawCare account

This information is associated with your PawCare account and is used to provide account and application functionality.

2. Pet Information

PawCare allows you to create and manage profiles for your pets.

Depending on the information you choose to provide, pet profiles may contain:

  • Pet name
  • Species
  • Breed
  • Age
  • Weight
  • Pet status
  • Pet profile image
  • Other pet-related information you voluntarily provide

Pet profile information is stored in PawCare's backend database and is associated with your user account.

3. Medication Information

PawCare allows you to record and manage medication information for your pets.

This may include information such as:

  • Medication name
  • Dosage information
  • Medication schedule
  • Start and end dates
  • Medication notes
  • Other medication-related information entered by you

This information is used to help you organize your pet's medication and provide relevant reminders.

4. Schedule and Reminder Information

PawCare allows you to create and manage schedules and reminders for your pets.

Schedule information may include:

  • Reminder titles
  • Dates
  • Times
  • Medication schedules
  • Pet-care activities
  • Notes
  • Other schedule information entered by you

This information is used to provide scheduling and reminder functionality.

5. Health Timeline Information

PawCare provides a timeline feature for organizing your pet's health and care history.

Information entered into the health timeline may include:

  • Health-related events
  • Veterinary visits
  • Treatments
  • Notes
  • Dates
  • Other pet-care information entered by you

This information is stored as part of your pet's records and is used to provide the application's timeline functionality.

6. Veterinary Contact Information

PawCare may allow you to save veterinary contact information for easier access.

This may include information such as:

  • Veterinarian or clinic name
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Contact details
  • Notes

This information is provided voluntarily by you and is stored to provide the veterinary-contact functionality.

You should only add contact information that you are authorized to store and use.

7. Profile and Pet Images

PawCare provides image-upload functionality.

Profile Images

Profile images may be stored in the profile-avatar storage bucket.

Pet Images

Pet images may be stored in the pet-images storage bucket.

Uploading a profile picture or pet picture is optional.

Images are used to display your profile or pet profile within the application.

8. Supabase

PawCare uses Supabase as its backend infrastructure.

Supabase provides services used by PawCare for authentication, database storage, and file storage.

Depending on the feature, information may be stored using:

Supabase Authentication

Used to manage PawCare user accounts and authentication.

Supabase Database

PawCare currently uses database tables including:

  • profiles
  • pets
  • medications
  • schedule_events
  • timeline_events
  • vet_contacts

These tables store information necessary to provide PawCare's account, pet-care, medication, scheduling, timeline, and veterinary-contact features.

Supabase Storage

PawCare currently uses storage buckets including:

  • profile-avatar
  • pet-images

These storage locations are used for profile and pet images.

PawCare uses access controls and database security policies, including Row Level Security (RLS) where applicable, to help restrict access to user-specific information.

9. Google Sign-In

PawCare may provide Google Sign-In as an authentication option.

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide information necessary to authenticate your PawCare account, which may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Google account identifier
  • Other information made available through the authentication process

PawCare uses this information for account authentication and account management.

Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

10. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through PawCare to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide Google Sign-In
  • Create and manage pet profiles
  • Display profile and pet images
  • Store medication information
  • Manage medication schedules
  • Manage pet-care schedules
  • Provide reminders
  • Maintain pet health timelines
  • Store veterinary contact information
  • Provide application functionality
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Protect the security of the application
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

11. Notifications and Reminders

PawCare may provide notifications related to schedules and reminders created by you.

Notifications may relate to:

  • Medication schedules
  • Pet-care activities
  • Veterinary appointments
  • Checkups
  • Other scheduled events

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

12. Camera and Photo Access

If PawCare requests camera or photo-library permission, the permission may be used to allow you to select or capture profile or pet images.

You are not required to upload images in order to use PawCare's core pet-management features.

You can manage camera and photo permissions through your device settings.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored through PawCare against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security measures may include:

  • Authentication
  • Access controls
  • Supabase security mechanisms
  • Row Level Security (RLS)
  • Storage access policies
  • Secure communication between the application and backend services

However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Therefore, while we take reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee that information will always remain completely secure.

14. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your information may be processed by third-party services necessary to operate PawCare, including:

  • Supabase, for authentication, database, and storage infrastructure
  • Google, when you choose Google Sign-In

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law
  • Respond to valid legal requests
  • Protect the security of PawCare
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Protect the rights, safety, or property of users or others

15. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide PawCare's services or until you delete your account, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Information associated with your account may include:

  • Profile information
  • Pet information
  • Medication information
  • Schedule information
  • Timeline information
  • Veterinary contact information
  • Profile images
  • Pet images

When you delete your account, we will initiate deletion of information associated with your account according to our account-deletion process.

Certain information may temporarily remain in backups or systems where retention is technically necessary or required for security or legal purposes.

16. Account Deletion

PawCare provides an account deletion option within the application.

You can use the account deletion feature to request deletion of your PawCare account and associated information.

Depending on the information associated with your account, deletion may include:

  • Your profile
  • Your pet profiles
  • Pet images
  • Profile image
  • Medication records
  • Schedule events
  • Timeline events
  • Veterinary contact information
  • Other information associated with your account

Once information has been permanently deleted, it may not be recoverable.

Information stored temporarily in backups or systems subject to required retention periods may remain for a limited period before permanent deletion.

17. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for the information you enter into PawCare.

You should:

  • Provide accurate information where appropriate
  • Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information
  • Ensure that you have permission to upload images or contact information belonging to other people
  • Keep your account credentials secure
  • Notify us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization

18. Pet Health Disclaimer

PawCare is designed to help pet owners organize and manage pet-care information.

PawCare does not provide professional veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

Medication information, health timelines, reminders, and other information displayed by PawCare should not be considered a replacement for professional veterinary advice.

For serious or emergency conditions, users should contact a qualified veterinarian.

19. Children's Privacy

PawCare is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to PawCare inappropriately, they may contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.
