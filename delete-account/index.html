1. Delete Your Account

The primarily way to permanently delete your account is directly from inside the PawCare mobile application. Follow these steps:

  1. Open the PawCare app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open your Profile / Account Settings.
  4. Select "Delete Account".
  5. Review the deletion information.
  6. Confirm account deletion.
  7. PawCare will process the account deletion.

Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

2. What Data Is Deleted

Account deletion is intended to remove all account-associated data. This includes:

  • User profile information
  • Pet profiles
  • Pet images
  • Profile image
  • Medication records
  • Schedule and reminder events
  • Health timeline records
  • Veterinary contact information
  • Other account-associated information stored for the user's account

Database tables

Your information will be deleted from the following tables:

  • profiles
  • pets
  • medications
  • schedule_events
  • timeline_events
  • vet_contacts

Storage buckets

Associated images will be removed from:

  • profile-avatar
  • pet-images

3. Data Storage

PawCare uses Supabase for application backend services and data storage.

Your data is securely stored within Supabase to provide the core functionality of the application.

4. Privacy Information

PawCare may process information necessary to provide its features, including:

  • Account/profile information
  • Pet information
  • Medication information
  • Schedule/reminder information
  • Health timeline information
  • Veterinary contact information
  • Profile and pet images

This information is used exclusively to provide the application's pet health management features.

5. Google Sign-In

You may use Google Sign-In to authenticate your PawCare account. When using Google Sign-In, PawCare uses this service solely to authenticate your account and manage access, and does not receive unnecessary Google account information.
